# HIT YA! — Game Pitch

**Genre:** Hybrid-Casual | Arcade | Distance & Timing
**Platform:** Mobile | Portrait | One Thumb

## Core Loop

Launch a stick-man with a bat by setting the angle and power. Time taps upon landing to achieve **Perfect Bounces** (±6 frames), gaining extra distance and score. Missing a bounce ends the run, with an optional rewarded ad to continue.

## Meta — Earn, Spend & Unlock

- **Currency:** Earn coins from distance, quests, and achievements. Spend on upgrades and cosmetics.
- **Upgrades:** Improve bat power, bounce strength, and character bounciness.
- **Customization:** Unlock outfits and accessories through progression.
- **Worlds:** Progress from a black-and-white stick world to colorful environments with evolving music.
- **Shop:** Free progression with optional ads and currency purchases.

## Why Come Back?

1. **Daily Quests & Rewards:** Earn coins, temporary score multipliers, daily login rewards, and welcome-back gifts.
2. **Progression & Collection:** Beat distance records, unlock new worlds, upgrades, and cosmetics.

## Who & When?

Casual mobile players seeking quick, satisfying arcade gameplay during short breaks.
**Sessions:** 1–3 minutes.

## Feel — What Makes It Crunchy?

- **Impact:** Strong hit sounds, screen shake, animations, and particles.
- **Perfect Bounce:** Character glow, haptic feedback, and acceleration bursts.
- **Distance:** Increasing speed, evolving music, colorful backgrounds, and rising scores.

## Scope

**MVP:** Launch, flight physics, timed bouncing, scoring, basic upgrades, and feedback.

**Later Features:** Mid-air obstacles, collectible coins, boosts, new worlds, events, and expanded customization.
